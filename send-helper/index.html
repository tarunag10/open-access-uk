SEND basics

Exclusion process

Schools can exclude for fixed periods or permanently. Governors must review exclusions within 15 school days. A permanent exclusion triggers an automatic Independent Review Panel (IRP) hearing.

SEND tribunal

You can appeal to the First-tier Tribunal (SEND) about EHCP decisions, refusal to assess, or contents of an EHCP. You generally have 2 months from the mediation certificate or LA decision.

EHCP rights

Parents and young people have the right to request an EHCP assessment, appeal EHCP contents, and challenge LA decisions through mediation or tribunal.

Not legal advice
