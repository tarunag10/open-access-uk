How to complain to each regulator

GMC (Doctors)

No statutory time limit but prompt action is recommended. The GMC investigates through fitness-to-practise proceedings including initial assessment, investigation, MPTS hearing, and sanctions up to erasure.

SRA (Solicitors)

Within 6 years of the issue. The SRA investigates and may refer to the Solicitors Disciplinary Tribunal (SDT) which can impose sanctions from rebuke to striking off.

Legal Ombudsman

Within 6 months of the final response from the service provider. The Legal Ombudsman investigates service complaints including delays, poor communication, and billing issues.

ACCA / RICS / BPS

Within 5-6 years depending on regulator. Each has its own disciplinary process including preliminary assessment, investigation, committee hearing, and sanctions.

Not legal advice
