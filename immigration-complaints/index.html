Home Office complaint process

Home Office complaint

Submit a written complaint to the Home Office relevant department. They must acknowledge within 5 working days and provide a full response within 20 working days.

ICIBI escalation

If the Home Office response is unsatisfactory, you can escalate to the Independent Chief Inspector of Borders and Immigration (ICIBI) for an independent investigation.

Parliamentary Ombudsman

If still unresolved, complain to the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman within 6 months of the ICIBI or final Home Office response.

Not legal advice
