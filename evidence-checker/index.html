Tribunal-specific requirements

File size limits

Each tribunal has a maximum file size. SSCS: 10MB, County Court: 20MB, Employment and FTT: 25MB, Housing: 15MB.

Accepted formats

Most tribunals require PDF. FTT and Employment also accept DOCX. Always check tribunal-specific guidance.

Not legal advice

This is a checking tool, not legal advice. Verify requirements against the current HMCTS guidance for your specific tribunal.
