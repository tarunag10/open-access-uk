How ombudsmen work

Independent investigation

Ombudsmen are independent bodies that investigate complaints about organisations. They can make binding decisions and recommend compensation or action.

Free service

Most ombudsman services are free to use. You must usually complain to the organisation first before escalating to the ombudsman.

Time limits

Each ombudsman has time limits for complaints. Some allow 6 months, others up to 12 months. Check the specific ombudsman's guidance for deadlines.

Not legal advice

This is an informational tool, not legal advice. Verify routes, deadlines, and rights against the current ombudsman guidance and legal requirements.
