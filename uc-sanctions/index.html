UC sanctions process

Sanction types

Higher-level: 100% standard allowance deduction for up to 26 weeks. Standard: 20% deduction for up to 4 weeks. Lower-level: deduction equivalent to missed appointment.

Mandatory Reconsideration

You have 1 month from the date of the sanction decision to request a Mandatory Reconsideration. This is the first step before appealing to an independent tribunal.

Good reasons

Hospital appointments, caring responsibilities, transport failure, mental health episodes, and interviews running late are all recognised good reasons for non-compliance.

Hardship payments

If you are a UC claimant with an active sanction and cannot meet your basic needs (rent, food, heating), you may be eligible for an advance hardship payment.

Not legal advice

This is a challenge-building aid, not legal advice. Verify deadlines, routes, and rights against the current DWP and GOV.UK guidance.
