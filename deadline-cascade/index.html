How to use the cascade view

Pick a template

Choose a process template that matches your situation. Each template has pre-configured steps with the correct working day and calendar day offsets.

Set your start date

Enter the date you submitted your request or complaint. The tool calculates every subsequent deadline from this date, accounting for working days where required.

Read the timeline

Green steps are completed. Orange is your current step. Grey are upcoming. Red means overdue. The progress bar shows overall completion.

Not legal advice

This is a tracking aid, not legal advice. Verify deadlines, routes, and rights against the current legislation and guidance for your process.
