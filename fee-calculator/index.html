Court and tribunal fees

How fees work

HMCTS sets fixed fees for different court and tribunal claims. The fee depends on the type of claim and the amount you are claiming. Fees must be paid when you issue or start proceedings.

Help with fees

If you are on a low income or receiving qualifying benefits, you may not have to pay court fees. Apply using form EX160 before or at the time of filing.

Exemptions

Some people are automatically exempt from fees, including asylum seekers, victims of domestic violence, and those receiving certain benefits.

No-fee employment tribunal

Employment tribunal claims have been fee-free since 2017 following the Supreme Court ruling in R (UNISON) v Lord Chancellor. No fee is required to bring or respond to an employment tribunal claim.

Not legal advice

This is an estimate tool, not legal advice. Verify fees against the current HMCTS fee schedules and seek professional advice for complex cases.
