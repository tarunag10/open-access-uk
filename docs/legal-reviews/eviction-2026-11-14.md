# Eviction checklist review: Ground 8, Ground 14 and transition

Research date: 14 November 2026. England only. This is a preliminary checklist,
not a determination of notice validity, possession entitlement or court outcome.

## Scope and implemented rules

- Require express confirmation of an assured tenancy in England that is not a
  social housing assured tenancy with a private registered provider (PRP).
  A PRP's non-social-housing letting is not excluded just because of its landlord.
  Unknown and out-of-scope tenancies cannot return a clean checklist result.
- Ground 8: at service and hearing, three months' lawful rent if payable monthly;
  thirteen weeks if payable weekly or fortnightly. The latter is 6.5 fortnightly
  payments, not thirteen fortnightly payments. Compare monetary amounts, not a
  guessed conversion of elapsed months. Deduct only unpaid rent attributable
  solely to non-receipt of an entitled Universal Credit award including housing.
  Require an explicit zero where there is no qualifying amount. An unknown
  hearing position is a request for information, not proof the legal ground fails.
- Saved pre-May rules: two months/eight weeks for supported payment frequencies.
  Quarterly/yearly rent remains outside scope. For pre-1 May 2026 section 8
  notices, check the date the claimant requested the court to issue the claim,
  not the later administrative issue date. Request must precede the twelve-month
  anniversary of service and be no later than 31 July 2026, subject to statutory
  savings and validity of the original notice. Timely pending proceedings may
  preserve the old regime until concluded. The user must inspect the court record.
- Ground 14: retain immediate proceedings after service. In the ordinary served
  notice, Ground-14-only case, possession cannot take effect during the fourteen
  days beginning with service. Example: service 1 May gives earliest effect
  15 May. This is neither a fourteen-day notice waiting period nor the date the
  court makes the order. Section 7(5D) also covers only-established-7A/14 cases;
  mixed grounds and dispensation of notice need specialist review and are not
  calculated by this single-ground workflow.
- Existing Section 21 transition input is relabelled as claim request date too.
  This patch does not purport to implement every historic Section 21 validity
  condition, expiry exception or saving. Historical stored issue dates are not
  silently reinterpreted as claim request dates.

## Verification limits

Official legislation data.html pages were inspected through web retrieval,
including the relevant amendment/commencement annotations. Direct raw downloads
returned HTTP 202 WAF challenges with empty bodies; XHTML status pages were not
supported by the web reader. No challenge was solved. A raw-byte statutory audit,
quote verification, and exhaustive outstanding-amendment check could not be
completed. This note paraphrases rather than quotes legislation. Maintainer/legal
review remains required before publication. No claim is made that every rule of
possession law is encoded or that all tenancy-specific transition questions can
be resolved by dates alone.
