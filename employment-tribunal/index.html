Employment tribunal process

ACAS early conciliation

Before submitting an ET1, you must contact ACAS for early conciliation. This is free and can help resolve your dispute without a tribunal hearing.

ET1 claim form

If conciliation fails, you can submit an ET1 claim form to the employment tribunal. You have 3 months from the date of dismissal (or act complained of) to submit.

Time limits

Most claims must be submitted within 3 months. Some claims like breach of contract and redundancy have a 6-month limit. Always check the specific time limit for your claim type.

Not legal advice

This is a case building aid, not legal advice. Verify deadlines, routes, and rights against the current employment tribunal guidance and seek professional legal advice if needed.
