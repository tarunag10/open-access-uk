NHS complaints process

PALS first

Most hospitals have a Patient Advice and Liaison Service (PALS). PALS can usually resolve concerns informally within 25 working days.

Formal complaint

If PALS cannot resolve your concern, you can make a formal written complaint to the trust. They must acknowledge within 3 working days and respond within 25 working days.

PHSO escalation

If you are not satisfied with the trust's response, you can complain to the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman within 12 months.

Not legal advice

This is a tracking aid, not legal advice. Verify deadlines, routes, and rights against the current NHS England and PHSO guidance.
