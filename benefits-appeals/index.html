Appeals guidance

        

          

            
Mandatory reconsideration

            

              You must request a mandatory reconsideration (MR) within 1 month of the decision date.
              DWP will review the decision and issue an MR notice.
            

          

          

            
Tribunal appeal

            

              If MR is refused, you have 1 month from the MR notice date to appeal to the First-tier
              Tribunal (Social Entitlement Chamber) using form SSCS1.
            

          

          

            
PIP descriptors

            

              PIP is scored on daily living and mobility descriptors. Points determine whether you
              qualify for the standard or enhanced rate of the daily living and/or mobility
              component.
            

          

          

            
Not legal advice

            

              This is a drafting aid, not legal advice. Verify deadlines and processes against
              current GOV.UK guidance. For independent advice, contact Citizens Advice or CPAG.
