Equality Act 2010 and accessible formats

Equality Act 2010

Under the Equality Act 2010, public authorities must make reasonable adjustments to ensure disabled people are not placed at a substantial disadvantage. This includes providing information in accessible formats.

How to request

Contact the organisation directly and request the format you need. You do not need to provide medical evidence. Specify the format, the documents you need, and a reasonable deadline.

RNIB and Mencap routes

For specialist formats like braille, large print, and audio, contact RNIB. For easy read formats, contact Mencap. For BSL, contact RNID.

Not legal advice

This is a request generator, not legal advice. Verify your rights and the organisation's obligations against the current Equality Act 2010 guidance.
